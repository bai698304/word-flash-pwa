# 英文标注词汇解析与学习

**Turns**: 3

---

## Turn 1


1. **disable**
  - **原解析**：原意为“使残废、使失去能力/使失效”。在文中指监视器切断动力，使汽车无法行驶/停止运转。
  - **原图句子**："There will be television chat shows hosted by robots, and cars with pollution monitors that will disable them when they offend."


2. **offend**
  - **原解析**：原意为“冒犯”，此处指汽车排污超标/违反环保规定。


1. **rental / vacancy (rental vacancy rate)**
  - **原解析**：`rental` 为“租赁的、租用的”；`vacancy` 指“空缺、空位”；`rental vacancy rate` 指“房屋出租空置率”。
  - **原图句段**："...C 讲 rental vacancy rate..."


2. **holds**
  - **原解析**：高频观点表达词，相当于 `believe` / `think`，意为“持……观点，认为”。
  - **原图句子**："The author holds that this year's increase in rail passengers fares..."


3. **CPI**
  - **原解析**：全称 Consumer Price Index（消费者物价指数），指物价指数/通货膨胀指标。
  - **原图句段**："...still well above the official CPI"


4. **retired**
  - **原解析**：形容词“退休的”；`retirees` 指“退休人员”。
  - **原图句子**："As a result of President Bush's reform, retired people may have..."


5. **retirees trading much or all of their guaranteed payments...**
  - **原解析**：核心结构 `trade A for B`，指“退休人员拿大部分或全部有保障的应付款项，去换取取决于投资回报的收益”。
  - **原图句段**："...with retirees trading much or all of their guaranteed payments for payments depending on investment returns..."


6. **New England**
  - **原解析**：新英格兰（美国东北部的一个地理区域名）。
  - **原图句子**："Which of the following is true of New England?"


7. **funding**
  - **原解析**：资金；拨款；资助（不可数名词）。
  - **原图句段**："...B 讲 funding..."


1. **evolutionary suicide**
  - **原解析**：演化上的自毁/进化走向死胡同。
  - **原图句段**："...Being a man has always been dangerous -> evolutionary suicide -> evolution is over..."


2. **hot spots**
  - **原解析**：指“热点问题、热门领域”（或地球物理学中的热点/地幔柱）。
  - **原图句段**："...the significance of hot spots in geophysical studies"


3. **geophysical**
  - **原解析**：地球物理（学）的。


4. **distrust**
  - **原解析**：不信任、怀疑。
  - **原图句子**："Why do so many Americans distrust what they read in their newspapers?"


5. **leap**
  - **原解析**：跳跃。谚语 Look before you leap 意为“三思而后行”。
  - **原图句子**："Look before you leap"


6. **futurist poetry**
  - **原解析**：未来派诗歌。
  - **原图句段**："...futurist poetry -> 排除A/D..."


7. **merits**
  - **原解析**：优点、长处、价值。
  - **原图句段**："...C选项 merits 是正面词..."


1. **courts / side with / defendants**
  - **原解析**：`court` 法院；`side with` 支持/偏向；`defendant` 被告。整句意为“一些法院开始偏向被告”。
  - **原图句段**："...some courts are beginning to side with defendants..."


2. **injury claims**
  - **原解析**：人身伤害索赔 / 赔偿请求。
  - **原图句段**："...some injury claims were no longer supported by law"


3. **supported by law**
  - **原解析**：得到法律的支持/保护。


4. **prejudices**
  - **原解析**：偏见、成见。`anti-colonial prejudices` 即“反殖民偏见”。
  - **原图句段**："...get over their outdated anti-colonial prejudices with respect to foreign investment..."


5. **thrive**
  - **原解析**：繁荣、茁壮成长、猖獗/蔓延。
  - **原图句子**："But one insidious form continues to thrive: alphabetism"


6. **alphabetism**
  - **原解析**：按字母顺序产生的歧视/偏见。


7. **inquiring minds**
  - **原解析**：有求知欲/爱思考的头脑（或人）。
  - **原图句段**："...inquiring minds are more important than scientific experiments"

## Turn 2


1. **equine 与 equids（顶栏手写对比）**
  - **解析**：`equine` 作形容词指“马的、马科的”，作名词指“马/马科动物”；`equids` 是名词复数，指“马科动物”（含马、驴、斑马等）。二者在文中互为同义词替换。
  - **原图句子**："...Samantha Brooks, an equine researcher at the University of Florida, says in a statement."


2. **critical（批注：有好处?）**
  - **解析**：**需要修正。**`critical` 在此处并非普通的“有好处”（beneficial），而是 **“至关重要的、极重要的”**（相当于 `crucial` / `essential`）。
  - **原图句子**："For thousands of years, donkeys have been critical for propelling human civilizations forward."


3. **propelling（批注：推动）**
  - **解析**：动词 `propel` 意为 **“推动、推进”**。


4. **intertwined（批注：交织/纠缠）**
  - **解析**：意为 **“紧密缠绕、交织在一起、息息相关”**。
  - **原图句子**："But where and when these animals first became intertwined with humans has been a mystery."


5. **tamed（批注：驯服）**
  - **解析**：动词 `tame` 意为 **“驯服、驯化”**（文中相当于 `domesticate`）。
  - **原图句段**："...about 3,000 years before humans tamed horses."


6. **journal（批注：期刊）**
  - **解析**：在学术语境下指 **“学术期刊、专业杂志”**。
  - **原图句子**："The team published their findings in the journal Science this month."


7. **written accounts（批注：书面记录）**
  - **解析**：意为 **“文字记载、书面记录”**。
  - **原图句段**："We usually only get the human's side of history through written accounts..."


8. **testimony（批注：证据/证明）**
  - **解析**：意为 **“证词、证据、见证”**。
  - **原图句段**："...we get a biological testimony to the environment these animals lived in and the experiences they survived."


9. **survived（批注：幸存）**
  - **解析**：意为 **“存活下来、经历……后幸存”**。


10. **equids（批注：马科动物）**
  - **解析**：名词，指 **“马科动物”**。
  - **原图句段**："They also looked at genomes from 15 wild equids and 31 earlier donkeys..."


11. **pinpoint（批注：精准定位）**
  - **解析**：意为 **“准确确定、精准指出”**。
  - **原图句段**："...used computer models to pinpoint the domestication event..."


12. **asses（批注：野驴/驴）**
  - **解析**：在动物学/历史语境下，`ass` 指 **“驴、野驴”**（`wild asses` 即野生驴）。


13. **traced（批注：追溯）**
  - **解析**：意为 **“追溯、跟踪……的来源/轨迹”**。
  - **原图句段**："They then traced how the animals spread across the rest of the continent..."


14. **coincided（批注：巧合/同时发生）**
  - **解析**：搭配 `coincide with` 意为 **“与……同时发生、巧合”**。
  - **原图句段**："...Freda Kreier reports that the event coincided with the Sahara growing larger and drier."


15. **enlisted（批注：征召/利用）**
  - **解析**：`enlist help` 意为 **“寻求/借助/利用（……的）帮助”**。
  - **原图句段**："Prehistoric humans may have enlisted donkeys' help in navigating the expanding Sahara."


16. **alters（批注：改变）**
  - **解析**：意为 **“改变、变更”**。
  - **原图句段**："...as climate change alters the planet's environment, write the authors."


1. **21题 B选项 underwent（批注：经历/遭受?）**
  - **解析**：动词 `undergo`（过去式 `underwent`）意为 **“经历、经受”**。
  - **原图选项**："[B] They underwent multiple domestication events."


2. **22题 题干 conveyed（批注：传达了想法?）**
  - **解析**：动词 `convey` 意为 **“传达、表达（信息/思想）”**。
  - **原图题干**："22. What message is conveyed in Brooks' statement?"


3. **23题 A选项 dispersed（批注：分散、散开?）**
  - **解析**：动词 `disperse` 意为 **“分散、散布、广泛分布”**。
  - **原图选项**："[A] dispersed widely in the world."
